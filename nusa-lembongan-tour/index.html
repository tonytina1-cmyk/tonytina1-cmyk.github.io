Nusa Lembongan FAQ

Can we visit Dream Beach and Devil’s Tear?

Yes. They are popular Lembongan stops and can be included in an island itinerary depending on your timing.

Can we include the Yellow Bridge?

Yes. The Yellow Bridge linking Nusa Lembongan and Nusa Ceningan is a popular sightseeing stop.

Can we snorkel on the same day?

Snorkelling can often be included, depending on sea conditions, availability and how much sightseeing you want to do.
