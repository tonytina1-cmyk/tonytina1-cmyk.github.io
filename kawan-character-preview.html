Design brief — Kawan:
Friendly, stylised 3D young Balinese man; warm tan skin, expressive brown eyes, naturally wavy dark hair, gentle smile, very light stubble, dark green and gold patterned Balinese udeng, white frangipani behind his ear, cream linen tropical-leaf shirt. Balanced appealing proportions like a polished animated film character, not a photorealistic deepfake or a blocky mannequin. Full body, A-pose for rigging, separate movable arms, legs and jaw, blinking eyelids and mouth shapes for future voice. Clean materials and embedded textures. Include idle and wave animation clips. Export as rigged GLB, ideally under 8 MB.
The next step: create Kawan's real 3D character
  1. Open MetaPerson Creator and choose its stylised/cartoon character option. Try the approved Kawan portrait as a reference; if it cannot use an illustration, start with a preset.
  2. Match the warm smile, wavy dark hair, brown eyes, headband and cream tropical shirt. Review the model by rotating it before exporting.
  3. Export as GLB with the free standard settings (if offered). Keep the original file so we can add waving, facial motion and voice later.
  4. Choose the exported GLB below to preview it here. Do not place it on the customer homepage until the face, outfit and proportions are approved.
Open 3D character creator Alternative: free 3D editor

MetaPerson currently advertises a free first standard rigged model export, but creating it needs an external account. Free exports may not include animation clips; we can add them later.
