BEFORE YOU BOOK

A few helpful answers

How do I book with Budi?

Choose a tour or service, then send Budi your travel date, group size and pickup area by WhatsApp or email. Confirm the quote and trip details with him before your day.

Can I customise my day?

Yes. Share the places you would like to visit and your preferred pace. Budi can help plan a practical route around your group, travel time and available options.

Where will I be picked up?

Send your hotel name or pickup address when you enquire. Confirm the meeting point, pickup time and any location-related costs with Budi before booking.

What is included in the price?

Inclusions depend on the tour or service you choose. Ask Budi to confirm transport, entry tickets, activities, boat transfers and any other costs in your quote.

How do payment and booking changes work?

Confirm the payment method, timing and any deposit with Budi when you book. Ask about cancellation or date-change terms before confirming your plans.
