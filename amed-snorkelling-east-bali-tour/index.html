Amed Tour FAQ

Can beginners snorkel in Amed?

Some Amed areas are commonly used by snorkellers, but conditions vary. Ask Budi about the best option for your group and use a local snorkelling guide if needed.

Can we visit Jemeluk Bay?

Yes. Jemeluk Bay can be the main snorkelling stop for this tour.

Can we add Lipah Beach?

Yes, if time and sea conditions suit your route.

Is snorkelling equipment included?

No. Equipment or local guide costs are separate unless Budi confirms a package with you.
