North Bali & Bedugul FAQ

How long is a North Bali and Bedugul tour?

It is usually a longer driving day than a local Ubud or South Bali tour. The total time depends on your pickup location and chosen stops.

Can we visit Ulun Danu Beratan Temple?

Yes. Ulun Danu Beratan Temple can be included in a Bedugul itinerary depending on your chosen route and timing.

Can waterfalls be included?

Yes. Waterfall options can be added depending on the route, weather, walking conditions and how much time you want to spend at each stop.

Is Bedugul cooler than South Bali?

Bedugul is in Bali’s highlands and generally feels cooler than the southern coastal areas.
