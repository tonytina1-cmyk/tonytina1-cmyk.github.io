What to think about before booking

Your designHave a reference, style or general idea ready. The tattoo artist should confirm the final design directly with you.
Size & placementApproximate size and body placement help the studio understand the appointment you may need.
Your travel scheduleConsider swimming, sun exposure and other activities after your tattoo when choosing your appointment date.
AftercareFollow the tattoo artist’s aftercare instructions carefully and ask questions before leaving the studio.

Important: tattoo suitability, design, hygiene procedures, pricing and aftercare are matters to confirm directly with the tattoo studio or artist. TBT’s role is to help with the experience, coordination and private transport.

Bali tattoo experience FAQ

Can Budi take me to and from the tattoo studio?

Yes. Private transport can be arranged around your hotel or villa location and appointment time.

Can I send a tattoo idea before the appointment?

Yes. Send your general idea, reference image, preferred size and placement so the studio can better understand what you are looking for.

Is the tattoo price included in transport?

Not automatically. Tattoo pricing depends on the design, size, placement and studio. Confirm the tattoo price and transport arrangements before booking.

Can I combine the tattoo appointment with other plans?

Sometimes. It depends on the appointment length, location and what you want to do before or after. Budi can help work out a sensible schedule.

Interested in a Bali tattoo experience?

Once you’ve looked through the details, send Budi your travel date, hotel area and tattoo idea. He can help with the next steps and transport plan.
