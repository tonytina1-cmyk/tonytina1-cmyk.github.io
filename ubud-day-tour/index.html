Ubud private driver FAQ

Can I customise my Ubud day tour?

Yes. Tell Budi the places you would like to see and he can help plan a practical route around your interests and available time.

Is the Ubud tour private?

Yes. The vehicle and driver are for your booking, so you can travel at your own pace rather than following a large group tour.

What can I visit on an Ubud day tour?

Popular choices include Tegallalang Rice Terrace, Tirta Empul Temple, Ubud Monkey Forest, waterfalls, craft villages and other cultural stops around Central Bali.

Can Budi pick me up outside Ubud?

Pickup can be arranged from many Bali areas. Send your hotel or villa location on WhatsApp so Budi can confirm the best pickup plan.

How do I book an Ubud private driver?

Send Budi a WhatsApp message with your date, pickup location, group size and the places you are interested in visiting.
