How the experience can work

1. Hotel pickup
Budi collects you from your accommodation.
2. Local market
Browse local produce and learn about common Balinese ingredients.
3. Cooking class
Join a guided hands-on class and prepare a selection of Balinese dishes.
4. Enjoy the meal
Sit down together and enjoy what you have prepared before returning to your hotel.

What to know

Cooking class fees are separate unless Budi confirms a package. Class menus, start times and dietary options vary by provider. Tell Budi about allergies or dietary requirements when booking so the right class can be arranged.

Cooking Class FAQ

Can children join?

Many cooking classes welcome families, but age limits can vary. Budi can help check the best option for your group.

Are vegetarian options available?

Often yes, but dietary requirements should be confirmed before booking.

Is transport included?

Budi can arrange private pickup and return transport as part of the TBT experience.
