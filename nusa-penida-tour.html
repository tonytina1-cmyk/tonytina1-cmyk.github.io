Popular West Nusa Penida highlights

Broken BeachA natural coastal arch and circular cove that is a popular stop on West Nusa Penida itineraries.

A smoother way to plan your Nusa Penida day

Nusa Penida looks compact on a map, but road conditions and travel times can make a rushed itinerary tiring. A focused route gives you more time at the viewpoints and less time trying to squeeze too much into one day.

West route, East route or a custom plan?

First-time visitors often choose the West Nusa Penida route for Kelingking Beach, Broken Beach and Angel’s Billabong. East Nusa Penida has a different set of viewpoints and beaches. If you already have a list of places, send it to Budi and ask what can realistically fit into your available time.

Best advice: do not try to pack every famous Nusa Penida stop into one day. A realistic itinerary is usually more enjoyable than spending most of the day in the car.

What can we see on a Nusa Penida day tour?

A popular West Nusa Penida route includes Kelingking Beach, Broken Beach and Angel’s Billabong, with Crystal Bay or another stop added when timing allows.

Private island transport can be arranged for your booking. Confirm your group size and package details with Budi before travel.

Can we visit both West and East Nusa Penida in one day?

Trying to cover both sides in one day can mean a lot of driving. A focused West or East route is usually more comfortable, depending on the places you most want to see.
