Frequently asked questions about Bali private drivers and tours

Planning your Bali holiday? Here are answers to common questions about private drivers, airport transfers and custom tours with TBT Tours & Travel by Budi Bali Driver.

Can I book a private driver in Bali?

Yes. TBT Tours & Travel by Budi Bali Driver offers private Bali driver services for custom day trips and sightseeing. Contact Budi with your date, pickup location, group size and preferred stops for a quote.

Does TBT offer Bali airport transfers?

Yes. TBT arranges private transfers to and from Bali's Ngurah Rai International Airport. Share your flight details, destination and passenger count when enquiring.

Which Bali destinations can I visit?

TBT offers trips including Ubud, Uluwatu, Kintamani and Mount Batur, North and East Bali, plus island excursions such as Nusa Penida and Nusa Lembongan. Ask Budi about a custom itinerary.

Can I arrange a multi-day Bali itinerary?

Yes. Tell Budi your travel dates, accommodation locations and interests. TBT can discuss a custom multi-day plan and available drivers.

How do I request a quote?

Use the booking enquiry page or message Budi on WhatsApp. Prices and availability depend on the route, dates, group size and services requested.
