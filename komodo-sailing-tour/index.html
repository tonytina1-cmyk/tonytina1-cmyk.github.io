Set sail from Labuan Bajo on Princess Nabila, a traditional-style 33-metre liveaboard with seven comfort cabins. Choose a short island getaway or a longer cruise, with time for spectacular viewpoints, beaches, marine life and unforgettable sunsets.

Before you book: This is a Komodo/Flores sailing experience, not a Bali day tour. Sailing schedules, cabin availability, National Park access and all prices must be reconfirmed with Budi and the boat operator. The supplied brochure was valid for August–December 2026.

2 Days / 1 Night

Komodo Highlights

Day 1: Pickup in Labuan Bajo (brochure indicates 10:30–11:55 am, guide to confirm). Cruise to Kelor Island, Manjarite, Strawberry Rock and Kalong Island for sunset flying-fox viewing.

Day 2: Padar Island / Gili Lawa (dependent on availability and conditions), Komodo Island, Pink Beach, Manta Point and Taka Makassar, before returning to Labuan Bajo. The brochure gives an approximate return window of 7:00–8:30 pm.

What’s included?

Supplier package lists

  • Liveaboard boat accommodation and meals (breakfast, lunch and dinner)
  • Round-trip Labuan Bajo airport/hotel/meeting-point transfer
  • Free-flow mineral water, tea and coffee, snacks and soft drinks
  • Snorkelling gear (mask and snorkel; no fins)
  • Bed linen and bath towel
  • Local guide, basic first-aid kit and documentation
  • Wi-Fi via Starlink, subject to service availability
  • Kayak/canoe, paddle board and bean-bag lounging facilities listed onboard

Not included / confirm before booking

  • Komodo National Park entry and ranger fees
  • Personal expenses
  • Flights to or from Labuan Bajo
  • Any extras or services not confirmed in your written quote

Route changes or cancellations may occur due to sea conditions, weather or official restrictions. Ask Budi for the operator’s latest booking and cancellation terms.
