What the experience could look like

1. Hotel pickup: Travel with Budi or his driver team toward the Kintamani highlands.

2. Meet your cycling guide: Receive the operator's safety briefing and suitable equipment.

3. Downhill countryside ride: Follow the operator's guided route through villages and rural landscapes toward the Ubud area.

4. Return transfer: Your driver takes you back to your accommodation or onward destination.

Planning note: This is a proposed experience, subject to a suitable cycling partner. The exact route, riding duration, difficulty, age limits, helmets, guide, lunch, insurance, pickup areas and total price must be confirmed before booking. “Downhill” does not mean traffic-free or effortless; riders should be comfortable on a bicycle.
