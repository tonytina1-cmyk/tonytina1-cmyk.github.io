Mount Batur views and Bali highland scenery

Kintamani is one of Bali’s best-known highland areas, with wide views across the volcanic landscape toward Mount Batur and Lake Batur. The cooler air and mountain scenery make it a very different experience from the southern beach areas.

Is this the Mount Batur sunrise hike?

This page is for private driving and sightseeing around Kintamani and Mount Batur viewpoints. If you want a sunrise trekking or Jeep experience, ask Budi about the options available for your date.

Can Kintamani be combined with Ubud?

Yes. Depending on your pickup point and timing, Kintamani can often be combined with selected Central Bali stops such as rice terraces, temples, villages or coffee stops.

Is it cooler in Kintamani?

Kintamani is in Bali’s highlands and generally feels cooler than coastal areas, particularly in the morning and during wet weather.
