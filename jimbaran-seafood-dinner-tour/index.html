Jimbaran Seafood Dinner Tour FAQ

What is included in the experience?

Private transport with Budi, a visit to the local fish market and help arranging your seafood dinner on Jimbaran Beach.

Is dinner included in the price?

Seafood and restaurant charges are separate unless Budi confirms a package with you before the tour.

Can we choose our own seafood?

Yes. The market visit and dinner can be shaped around what your group likes and what is available that day.

Can children come?

Yes. The experience can be adapted for families and the timing can be discussed with Budi.

What time should we start?

The best start time depends on where you are staying and sunset time. Send Budi your hotel or pickup area and he can suggest a practical pickup time.
