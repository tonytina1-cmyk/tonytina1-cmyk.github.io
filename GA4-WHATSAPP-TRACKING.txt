TBT TOURS & TRAVEL - GA4 WHATSAPP TRACKING

Google Analytics property: TBT TOURS & TRAVEL
Measurement ID: G-9CV67QT4X3

EVENTS ADDED

1. whatsapp_click
   Records every WhatsApp link/button click on every website page.
   The homepage booking form also records one whatsapp_click when submitted.

2. enquiry_submit
   Records submission of the homepage Quick WhatsApp Booking form.
   No guest name, phone number, pickup location, date or notes are sent to Analytics.

MARK THE BOOKING ACTION AS A KEY EVENT

1. Open Google Analytics.
2. Open the TBT TOURS & TRAVEL property.
3. Go to Admin, then Key events.
4. Choose New key event.
5. Enter this exact event name: whatsapp_click
6. Save it.

Keep enquiry_submit as a normal event. This prevents one booking-form submission
from being counted twice as a booking conversion.

TEST AFTER NETLIFY DEPLOYMENT

1. Open the website in a private browser window.
2. Open Google Analytics Realtime.
3. Click one WhatsApp button.
4. Submit the Quick WhatsApp Booking form once.
5. Confirm whatsapp_click and enquiry_submit appear in Realtime.

Normal Analytics reports can take up to 24 hours to show new events.
